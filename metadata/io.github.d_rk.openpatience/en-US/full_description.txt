Open Patience is a relaxing, ad-free solitaire game — just you and the cards.

Included classics:

* Klondike (draw 1 and draw 3)
* FreeCell

Features:

* No ads, no tracking, no network access
* Undo and redo, with a timer and move counter
* Tap or drag to play; double-tap to send a card to its foundation
* Your game is saved automatically, so you can resume any time
